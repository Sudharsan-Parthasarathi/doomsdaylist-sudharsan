NEWBIE'S DOOMSDAY WATCHLIST

Don't have time for the entire MCU? Start here.

14 essential movies in watch order. Hover a poster to read the summary.

X-Men

Charles Xavier's mutants clash with Magneto's Brotherhood.

01

X-Men

2000 · Fox X-Men

Charles Xavier's mutants clash with Magneto's Brotherhood.

Why it matters: Introduces the Fox X-Men cast returning for Doomsday.

X2: X-Men United

Stryker's war on mutants forces rivals to team up.

02

X2: X-Men United

2003 · Fox X-Men

Stryker's war on mutants forces rivals to team up.

Why it matters: Often called the best of the originals.

Captain America: The First Avenger

Scrawny Steve Rogers becomes a super-soldier and fights HYDRA in WWII.

03

Captain America: The First Avenger

2011 · Phase One

Scrawny Steve Rogers becomes a super-soldier and fights HYDRA in WWII.

Why it matters: Sets the moral compass of the Avengers and introduces the Tesseract.

The Avengers

Earth's mightiest heroes unite to stop Loki's alien invasion.

04

The Avengers

2012 · Phase One

Earth's mightiest heroes unite to stop Loki's alien invasion.

Why it matters: The first team-up. Thanos appears in the mid-credits.

Avengers: Infinity War

Thanos hunts the six Infinity Stones and wipes out half of all life.

05

Avengers: Infinity War

2018 · Phase Three

Thanos hunts the six Infinity Stones and wipes out half of all life.

Why it matters: The pivot of the whole saga.

Avengers: Endgame

The surviving Avengers travel through time to undo the Snap.

06

Avengers: Endgame

2019 · Phase Three

The surviving Avengers travel through time to undo the Snap.

Why it matters: The Infinity Saga finale and Doomsday's emotional starting line.

Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings

Shang-Chi faces his father and the power of the Ten Rings.

07

Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings

2021 · Phase Four

Shang-Chi faces his father and the power of the Ten Rings.

Why it matters: Ends with an unanswered beacon. Shang-Chi is in Doomsday.

Spider-Man: No Way Home

Peter asks Strange to make the world forget he's Spider-Man, and the multiverse cracks open.

08

Spider-Man: No Way Home

2021 · Phase Four

Peter asks Strange to make the world forget he's Spider-Man, and the multiverse cracks open.

Why it matters: Brings in old Spider-Man villains and heroes.

Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness

Strange and America Chavez flee a grief-driven Scarlet Witch.

09

Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness

2022 · Phase Four

Strange and America Chavez flee a grief-driven Scarlet Witch.

Why it matters: Introduces incursions, the multiverse's biggest danger.

Black Panther: Wakanda Forever

Wakanda mourns T'Challa as Shuri faces the undersea kingdom of Talokan.

10

Black Panther: Wakanda Forever

2022 · Phase Four

Wakanda mourns T'Challa as Shuri faces the undersea kingdom of Talokan.

Why it matters: Shuri and Namor are both confirmed for Doomsday.

Deadpool & Wolverine

Deadpool recruits a reluctant Wolverine to save his universe from the TVA.

11

Deadpool & Wolverine

2024 · Phase Five

Deadpool recruits a reluctant Wolverine to save his universe from the TVA.

Why it matters: The bridge that lets the Fox X-Men appear in Doomsday.

Captain America: Brave New World

Sam Wilson steps up as Captain America against a new global threat.

12

Captain America: Brave New World

2025 · Phase Five

Sam Wilson steps up as Captain America against a new global threat.

Why it matters: Finishes Sam's journey. He's a major Doomsday character.

Thunderbolts*

A team of misfits is pulled into a deadly mission.

13

Thunderbolts*

2025 · Phase Five

A team of misfits is pulled into a deadly mission.

Why it matters: They become the New Avengers heading into Doomsday.

The Fantastic Four: First Steps

Marvel's First Family faces Galactus in a retro-futuristic 1960s.

14

The Fantastic Four: First Steps

2025 · Phase Six

Marvel's First Family faces Galactus in a retro-futuristic 1960s.

Why it matters: Doctor Doom's MCU debut and the direct setup for Doomsday.
