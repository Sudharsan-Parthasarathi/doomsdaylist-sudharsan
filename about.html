ABOUT ME

A little about the person behind the watchlist.

SUDHARSAN

I am a passionate Marvel fan who created this website to make the journey towards Avengers: Doomsday simple, clear, and enjoyable. With so many characters, stories, and connections across the MCU, this watchlist brings everything together in one place so that both longtime fans and newcomers can enjoy the story without feeling lost.

This project is a small way of sharing my love for Marvel and helping others discover the movies that make the road to Doomsday worth watching.
